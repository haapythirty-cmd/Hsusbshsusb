Who We Are

Brownsteel Fab is an Abuja-based welding and metal fabrication workshop. We design, fabricate and install stainless steel, mild steel and custom metal works for homes, estates, offices, hotels and commercial sites across the FCT and beyond.

From modern cable and glass stair railings to gates, grills, steel structures, stainless furniture and boutique shop fittings — every job is measured on site, built to last, and finished to a clean standard you can show a client.

2. Design & quote
3. Workshop fabricate
4. On-site install

Why Clients Call Us

Built for Abuja sites

We understand local building styles, granite and marble finishes, estate security needs and the weather that metalwork has to survive.

Clean finish, not rough workshop look

Stainless brushing, tight welds, aligned posts and level handrails. The work is meant to sit in living rooms, hotels and gated homes.

From sketch to installation

We take site measurements, agree the design, fabricate in the workshop and install. You are not left to find a second contractor.

Range of styles

Modern cable rails, frameless glass, classic gold-accent balusters, boutique shop fittings, gold and rose-gold displays, and stainless furniture.

Custom, not off-the-shelf only

If the drawing is unusual — floating treads, a tight landing, a restaurant set, a balcony return — we build it to the space.
